How the value math works (and the honest caveats)

Without the certificate, two of you pay full fare = 2 × ticket price. With it, you buy your own ticket and the companion flies for taxes & fees only — capped at about $80 round-trip domestic or $250 round-trip international (Mexico/Caribbean/Central America), for up to 4 segments.

Net savings = your fare − companion taxes. Across destinations, pricier eligible routes squeeze the most out of the cert — that’s how the value scan ranks them.

Both the Platinum and Reserve cards reach the same destinations — the U.S. (incl. Alaska & Hawaii), Mexico, the Caribbean, and Central America. They differ only by cabin: Platinum is Main Cabin (L/U/T/X/V); Reserve adds W/S Comfort+, A/G Premium Select, and I/Z First. Delta One and Basic Economy are never eligible. (The Gold card has no companion certificate.)

Booking class is the catch. The certificate only tickets if the flight has open inventory in an eligible class. The keyless Google Flights source shows real prices but not the booking class — so eligibility shows as “Confirm on Delta.” Add Amadeus keys (precise fare-class mode) to turn that into a firm eligible / not-eligible call. Either way, confirm the class when you book on delta.com.

Connect live Delta fares (Amadeus)

  1. Create a free account at developers.amadeus.com.
  2. Create an app → copy the API Key and API Secret.
  3. In the project folder, copy .env.example to .env and paste them: 
    AMADEUS_CLIENT_ID=your_key
AMADEUS_CLIENT_SECRET=your_secret
AMADEUS_ENV=test
  4. Restart the server (node server.js). Flip AMADEUS_ENV to production once your app is moved to production — the test dataset is thin for small airports like Helena.
